Rules & Regulations

Al-Hamd Chit
01
Monthly Payment Date

The monthly installment of ₹1,000 must be paid to the group leader between the 1st and 9th of every month.

02
Draw / Auction Time

The online draw will be conducted on the 10th of every month at 9:30 PM.

03
Fund Disbursement

The winning amount will be handed over to the winner within 10 days of the draw / auction.

04
Punctuality & Cooperation

Everyone is requested to pay the amount within the specified time. Those facing emergency situations should inform in advance.

05
Transparency

All monthly accounts will be updated in the group on the same day.

06
Special Note

The chit amount for the first month goes to the chit organizer.
